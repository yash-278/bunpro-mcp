# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate in issues, discussions, reviews, and pull requests.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, credential sharing, and attempts to access another person's Bunpro data are not acceptable. Respect Bunpro's service, keep research low volume, and use only accounts you are authorized to access.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary. Report sensitive conduct or security concerns through the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
